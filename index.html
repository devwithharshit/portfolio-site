01 / ABOUT ME

About Me

I'm Harshit Singh — an 18-year-old AI Enthusiast, Published Author, and Indie AI Content Creator on a mission to make complex AI ideas accessible through storytelling.

I believe the future belongs to creators who can bridge the gap between technology and human connection. I'm curious about how technology works, but I'm equally interested in how people think, create, and communicate.

I've published my book “Phases Unexpected” with Notion Press and launched Chronicles by Harshit, a space where I write about ideas, experiences, technology, and the things I'm still figuring out.

Through my YouTube channel, I share my learnings around AI, writing, and entrepreneurship while documenting my journey of building things and figuring things out along the way.

I've also worked on Chronicles Unfurled, explored ideas through Studio2Beyond, and continue to experiment with different ways of creating and sharing online.

I'm big on authenticity, curiosity, and building in public. I don't always have everything figured out, and honestly, I don't think I need to.

I write to reflect. I code to explain. I create to connect.
